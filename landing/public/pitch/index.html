02The problem

Structured notes are a bank-only product.

Issuance

Weeks to structure

Every note is a legal, operations and distribution project at a bank.

Fixings

Trust the calculator

The bank's own calculation agent decides what each investor is owed.

Credit

Unsecured issuer risk

Investors rely on the issuer's balance sheet, with nothing pledged to the note.

Access

Six-figure tickets

Sold to private-bank clients, only where the bank distributes.

And on-chain

Tokenized stocks now live on Robinhood Chain. Their holders can't raise against them without selling, and on-chain investors mostly get variable vault yields, not a term sheet.

03The solution

Issuers raise in minutes. Chainlink runs the note.

One note's life · Chainlink checks each date
  1. Issuer

    Collateralize & mint

    Set the terms, pledge collateral into the note, mint the units.

  2. Investors

    Subscribe

    USDG in, ERC-20 note units out. Proceeds fund the issuer.

  3. Chainlink CRE

    Fix & pay

    Strike, every observation and each payment step, on schedule.

  4. If a payment is missed

    Collateral pays

    Holders take the pledged collateral, up to its value.
